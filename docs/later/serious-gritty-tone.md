# Parked option: serious and gritty tone

*Parked 20/11/2026 (S01-DESIGN) at the director's request. The game starts with "grounded, light humour". This file keeps the darker version ready to pick up later, as a full mode, a difficulty setting, or a second game.*

## What would change

| Area | Grounded, light humour (current) | Serious and gritty (this option) |
|---|---|---|
| Mood | Believable medieval life with small funny moments | Hard life, hunger and fear are constant |
| Villager chatter | Jokes, gossip, odd orders from the chief | Short, tired lines; grief after raids |
| Death | You take over another villager who inherits your things | Same, but the heir remembers you: a short line about who died and how |
| Injuries | Health comes back with rest | Lasting wounds (limp = slower, bad arm = weaker hits) until treated |
| Food | Simple hunger bar | Winter shortages; the AI chief may ration food, and you can steal or share |
| War | Dangerous but not grim | Burned farms stay burned; refugees arrive; deserters are punished |
| Night | Raids and wolves | Also disease, theft, and choosing who to shelter |
| Colours | Warm, readable | Muted, darker nights, fog |
| Music | Light folk tunes | Sparse drums and strings, long silences |

## Mechanics this tone would add
- **Morale:** low morale slows work and makes you more likely to flee in battle.
- **Lasting wounds:** stay until a healer treats them; affect stats.
- **Hard choices at night:** shelter a stranger (could be a spy), share or hide food.
- **Desertion:** leaving the army mid-battle has a cost (lost reputation, fine, or jail).

## Cost and risk
- Mostly writing, colours and sound: cheap to add later if the systems are built with "tone" as data (text lines, colour palette, music set).
- Lasting wounds and morale are new systems: medium cost.
- Age rating would likely go up (see Phase 9).

## How to keep the door open now
- Keep all text lines in data files, grouped by tone.
- Keep the colour palette and music list swappable.
- Design health so wounds can be added later without rewriting combat.
